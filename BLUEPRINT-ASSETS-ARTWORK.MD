# BLUEPRINT-ASSETS-ARTWORK — ILLUSTRATION, KEY ART & ENVIRONMENTS

**Document ID:** MW-BP-ART-001 · **Version:** 1.0.0  
**Parent:** [BLUEPRINT-DESIGN-ASSETS.MD](./BLUEPRINT-DESIGN-ASSETS.MD) · **Task registry:** [tasks.json](./tasks.json)

## 1. Purpose and scope

Govern static and frame-based artwork: universe backgrounds, world environments, portal scenes, posters, illustrations, editorial imagery, maps, non-character effects and title cards. Character assets follow the character specification; logos/icons follow the vector specification.

Every artwork must serve a defined world, narrative moment, UI placement or communication objective. Do not generate art merely to fill empty screens.

## 2. World art direction

**ROCKSOUL — THE LIVING UNIVERSE:** cinematic futuristic spaces, portals, floating/layered environments, expressive energy and depth; original anime-game visual language without copying identifiable protected characters/assets. Composition leaves functional space for navigation and content. Use motion-ready layers only where useful.

**MOONWITNESS — WHEN THE MOON WITNESS, WE SHARE A MESSAGE:** lunar, investigative, editorial and discovery-oriented atmosphere. Visual metaphors for sources, timelines and evidence must not imply proof by appearance. Clarity takes priority over spectacle.

**STORY WORLDS:** each authored work has its own approved palette, composition, motif and emotional context. Do not merge separate works into one shared lore style without evidence.

**A-L-L-A-H.COM — EMPIRE OF THE LIGHT:** preserve exact taglines and known world names as text/metadata; preserve source references; keep the missing ninth-world identity unresolved.

## 3. Art brief contract

Every brief includes purpose/audience, world and exact source/canon reference, placement/crop, aspect ratio/responsive variants, focal subject/object and composition, palette/lighting, atmosphere/emotion, typography and exact copy, excluded elements/restrictions, formats/editable source expectation, provenance/rights and approval owner.

Prompts are not authority to invent canon. Mark speculative concepts as proposals.

## 4. Technical export standards

Keep editable masters (layered source where possible). Export optimized WebP/AVIF for web delivery, with PNG/SVG where transparency or sharp edges require it. Use responsive sizes, explicit dimensions, correct color profile, compression and content hash. Avoid oversized full-resolution files in browser bundles.

Manifest metadata: dimensions, MIME, world, asset ID, source, rights, alt/decorative flag, focal point, variants, version and checksum.

## 5. Composition and product fit

Test artwork in its real destination. Check overlay contrast; responsive safe areas; focal point at supported breakpoints; FX obstruction; loading/failure fallback; false operational/security signals; and redundant screen-reader output for decorative art.

## 6. Motion-ready artwork

Name layers by semantic purpose. Keep parallax/camera motion subtle, avoid rapid flashing, provide static fallback, respect reduced motion and keep text/interactive controls outside moving layers where possible. Never animate restricted depictions.

## 7. Review and rights

Review visual quality, narrative/canon accuracy, rights/provenance, safety boundaries, UI integration, accessibility and performance. Generated work is marked generated and linked to prompt/reference provenance; human approval is still required.

## 8. Restricted depictions

Do not create visual depictions of Allah, Prophet Muhammad or Isa/Jesus. Do not disguise such depictions as light entities, silhouettes, avatars, faceless characters or environmental figures. Preserve permitted textual references only in context. Ambiguous source material blocks generation/publication pending human review.

## 9. Deliverables and ownership

Deliver world art-direction boards, approved briefs, source masters, optimized responsive derivatives, asset manifest, crop/focal-point tests, rights/canon review, accessibility metadata and integration evidence.

Tasks **ART-001 through ART-008** in [tasks.json](./tasks.json).

## 10. Definition of done

Artwork has a documented purpose, correct world identity, editable source and optimized variants, safe/accessible composition, traceable rights/provenance, real UI integration and approved canon compliance.
