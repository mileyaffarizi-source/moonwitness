## 1. Purpose

This document governs the meaning, hierarchy, preservation, presentation and evolution of the platform's creative universe. It is the canonical narrative specification for product language, story structures, creative content, lore, world navigation and narrative experiences.

The platform is not one fictional story. It hosts distinct creative worlds, products, archives, stories, research experiences and operational capabilities. Shared infrastructure must not flatten their identities into one interchangeable theme.

## 2. Narrative hierarchy

Use this hierarchy as an organizing model unless original source material proves otherwise:

1. **THE LIVING PLATFORM** — technical and operational foundation.
2. **THE LIVING UNIVERSE** — conceptual frame connecting technology, creativity, intelligence, knowledge and experience.
3. **ROCKSOUL — THE LIVING UNIVERSE** — immersive experience and universe-entry language.
4. **MOONWITNESS — WHEN THE MOON WITNESS, WE SHARE A MESSAGE** — witness, research, evidence, knowledge and discovery universe.
5. **STORY WORLDS** — individual authored works, editions, characters, scenes and narrative timelines.
6. **A-L-L-A-H.COM — EMPIRE OF THE LIGHT** — distinct creative archive/world concept whose source canon must be preserved as authored.

This hierarchy does not grant permission to invent fictional connections, ownership, cosmology, chronology or character identities. A relationship not established by a source must be marked as a proposal or hypothesis.

## 3. Platform voice and narrative modes

Governing idea:

**One Universe. Three Realms. One Foundation. Independent Capabilities. Multiple Creative Worlds. One Coherent Experience.**

The voice should be confident, exploratory, emotionally aware, mysterious where appropriate and precise. Dramatic language must never replace factual clarity.

Use narrative modes deliberately:
- **Discovery:** invite investigation and progressively reveal context.
- **Witness:** make sources, provenance and known facts visible.
- **Mission:** show objectives, dependencies, progress and responsibility.
- **Story:** preserve authored voice, sequence, perspective and emotional context.
- **Threshold:** create a meaningful transition into an experience.
- **Operations:** communicate technical state clearly without hiding risk behind lore.
- **Governance:** use precise language for permissions, approvals, policy and evidence.

## 4. Canon Registry

Represent original narrative material through a versioned Canon Registry. Each record supports:
- immutable stable ID;
- exact original title and original text;
- content type and language;
- source file/URI/archive locator;
- author/origin when known;
- creation and revision dates when known;
- version, revision history and predecessor/successor;
- canon state: original, draft, in review, approved, published, archived, superseded, disputed or unverified;
- related world, work, edition, chapter, scene, character, event, motif, asset or timeline;
- relationship assertions and supporting evidence;
- rights, attribution and permitted use when known;
- editorial notes kept separate from original text.

Never overwrite original wording with cleaned-up, translated, summarized or AI-generated wording. Store derivatives as new records linked to their exact source version.

### 4.1 Relationship evidence

Every relationship stores relation type, source and target records, source evidence or explicit editorial decision, responsible actor, status (asserted/proposed/disputed/rejected/unverified), time context where applicable and change history.

An AI-discovered similarity is a proposal, not canon. Human review is required before publishing inferred relationships. Do not invent links to fill graph gaps.

## 6. Narrative and religious-content boundaries

Non-negotiable:
- Do not create visual depictions of Allah, Prophet Muhammad or Isa/Jesus.
- Preserve relevant names and textual references only in original authored context.
- Never translate a textual reference into a character, avatar, portrait, silhouette, stand-in or generated depiction.
- Do not infer sensitive identity, religious affiliation or historical identity from aliases.
- Keep quotation, interpretation, editorial framing and generated summaries visibly distinct.
- Apply boundaries to artwork, animation, characters, vector marks, icons, SFX metadata, prompts, thumbnails and previews.

If a task may breach a boundary, stop it, mark it blocked_policy and record the reason. Do not work around the restriction by changing the art style.

## 7. Narrative experience and content model

Suggested journey:

**THE THRESHOLD → UNIVERSE ENTRY → WORLD SELECTOR → WORLD EXPERIENCE → STORY / RESEARCH / MISSION / OPERATIONS**

A narrative page should expose the world/work, title and edition, reading position, exact authored text, source/provenance, factual status, relationships and chronology with uncertainty labels, related assets and revision history where relevant.

Do not force every work into one template. A poem, visual-effect study, research dossier, character archive and multi-chapter work need different compositions.

## 8. Localization and exact-string handling

Keep a source-language master. A translation is a linked derivative recording language, translator/agent, method, date, review state and source version. Protected brand strings and taglines retain exact spelling, punctuation and capitalization.

## 9. Human editorial authority

AI may index, tag, summarize, compare and propose. AI must not promote proposals into canon, rewrite source text in place, invent missing worlds/characters/chapters, claim unsupported verification, publish restricted material without approval or delete prior versions. Human canon/editorial owners approve canonical changes and publication.

## 10. Required deliverables

Source inventory and provenance report; canon taxonomy/schema; exact-string registry; unresolved-canon register; relationship evidence policy; editorial states and approval workflow; narrative information architecture; creative boundary tests; source-preserving import plan; traceability for implemented creative features.
